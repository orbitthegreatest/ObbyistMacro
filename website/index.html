The toolkit

Three macros. One hotkey each.

FPS Macro

Toggle the Roblox FPS cap between 60 and 240 with a single key. The app walks you through a guided 3-step calibration once, then automates the full menu navigation every time.

  • Guided calibration — remembers your menu layout
  • Keyboard or mouse button hotkey
  • Current cap always visible in the status bar

Wallhop Macro

A clean one-tap wallhop: 150° flick, jump, flick back — about 100 ms total. The flick distance is computed from your Roblox sensitivity, so it feels right on any setup.

  • Pixel-perfect flick from your sensitivity
  • 19 ms wallhop length (Spencer defaults)
  • One hotkey, zero spam

Freeze Macro

Freeze the entire Roblox process instantly (NtSuspendProcess) and resume just as fast — perfect for landing, cutting and re-centering your camera.

  • Toggle mode — one key, on and off
  • Hold mode — frozen only while held
  • Works with keyboard or mouse hotkey

How it works

Set up once. Play forever.

1

Install & launch

Run the installer. ObbyistMacro lives in your system tray and stays out of your way.

2

Calibrate the FPS tab

A guided tooltip walks you through the Roblox menu once. The app remembers exactly where the FPS option lives.

3

Bind your hotkeys

Click a keybox and press the key (or mouse button) you want. Esc cancels.

4

Play Roblox

Global hooks watch for your hotkeys — and they only fire while Roblox is the focused window.
